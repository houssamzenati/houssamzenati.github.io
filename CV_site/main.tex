\cvsection{Research Interests}{
I develop machine learning algorithms and statistical theory for learning from partial feedback, understanding intervention effects, and designing adaptive experiments. My research progresses from learning which interventions to choose, to establishing what they change, to deciding what to try next---when good decisions and informative experiments need not favor the same assignments.
}

{\bfseries Introduction to Causal Inference with Hidden Confounders: Instruments and Proxies}, UM6P \hfill 12 November 2025\\
\textbf{Guest lecture.} A 90-minute lecture followed by a separate 90-minute practical, progressing from potential outcomes and adjustment with observed covariates to instruments, proxy variables, and bridge functions for hidden confounding. \href{https://houssamzenati.github.io/assets/slides/causal-inference-hidden-confounders-um6p-2025.pdf}{Slides}.

\vspace{0.4em}

{\bfseries Causal Inference}, New York University Paris \hfill 2024--2025\\
\textbf{Co-instructor}, with Judith Abecassis. This course introduces the main tools of causal inference, from randomized experiments to observational and quasi-experimental methods, with a particular emphasis on observational inference.

\vspace{0.4em}

{\bfseries Advanced Machine Learning}, CentraleSup\'elec \hfill 2024--2025\\
\textbf{Teaching assistant}, alongside Emilie Chouzenoux. This course covers modern machine learning methods at the interface of statistics and optimization, with an emphasis on connecting theory, assumptions, and algorithmic practice.

\vspace{0.4em}

{\bfseries Data Visualisation}, Universit\'e Paris Dauphine -- PSL \hfill 2021--2022\\
\textbf{Instructor} for Master's students in the IASD program, presenting data visualisation as a statistical and algorithmic tool for exploring, summarizing, and communicating complex data.

\vspace{0.6em}

When teaching, I like to emphasize conceptual clarity, mathematical rigor, and active engagement, with the goal of helping students understand both the structure of a method and the assumptions that make it work.
