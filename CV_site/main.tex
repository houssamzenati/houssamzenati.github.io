\cvsection{Research Interests}{
I develop statistical theory and algorithms for reliable inference and decision-making with learned models. My work connects nuisance-robust inference, policy learning and adaptive experimentation: controlling the estimation errors that affect a target or decision, and collecting the observations needed to assess it. I am particularly interested in applying these ideas to biology and biomedicine.
}
